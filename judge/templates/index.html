Twelve weeks of ads measurement, as competitions

Each week is a problem from real ads ML — conversion modelling, calibration, delayed feedback, attribution, incrementality, bidding, privacy. Download the data, build a model, submit predictions, and see where you land.

Leaderboards rank on normalised entropy, not AUC. A model that ranks perfectly and is calibrated three times too high will win on AUC and lose money in an auction; NE sees both.
