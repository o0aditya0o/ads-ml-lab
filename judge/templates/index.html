The problems ads ML actually gets wrong

Conversion modelling, calibration, delayed feedback, attribution, incrementality, bidding, privacy — one competition per week, on Criteo's public logs. Download the split, build a model, submit predictions, land on a leaderboard.

Ranked on normalised entropy, not AUC. AUC is invariant to monotone rescaling: a model calibrated three times too high scores identically and would overbid every auction threefold. NE is a proper scoring rule — it sees ranking and calibration. Both are on the board, because where they disagree is the interesting part.
