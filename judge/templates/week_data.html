What was removed, and why

Columns that would leak the answer are stripped from the test file — not just {{ comp.target_column }} but conversion_timestamp, conversion_id and attribution. That last one is the subtle one: it is Criteo's own last-click flag and is nonzero only on converting journeys, so shipping it would hand over the label under a different name.

The split boundary is a timestamp quantile, taken before subsampling. Subsampling first and splitting second would let a test row predate a train row, which is the whole failure this competition exists to avoid teaching.
